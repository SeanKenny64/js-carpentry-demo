Our Services

Detailed information about the building and carpentry work we carry out across Bristol.

Roofing

We handle all aspects of roofing across Bristol, from minor repairs to full replacements. Much of Bristol's housing is over 120 years old, and we know exactly what to look for beneath the tiles.

  • Roof Renovations: Fitting new breathable membranes, battens, and repairing broken rafters or decayed timber.
  • Exterior Repairs: Fixing chimney stacks, lead flashings, parapet walls, and coping stones to keep your roof watertight.
  • Full Replacements: Complete replacement of cut roofs when necessary.

With extensive experience in Victorian and Georgian housing stock, we understand the specific challenges older Bristol properties face. Get in touch for a free estimate.

Extensions

We create light, airy extensions that transform how you use your home. Our work often involves forming new structural openings and knock-throughs to create open-plan kitchen and diner areas with seamless garden access.

We have extensive experience with Victorian and Georgian homes, but also take on complex, modern builds. For example, on a large ground-floor extension, we dug out part of the basement for extra headroom, moved the kitchen downstairs, installed a goalpost steel, and finished the flat roof with EPDM rubber and exposed beams.

Full Project Management: We can manage the entire build, working alongside tried-and-tested sub-contractors for rendering, plastering, electrical, plumbing, and structural work. All works are Architect-led and fully Building Control compliant.

Kitchens

We provide new kitchen installations, alterations, and re-location. We regularly fit kitchens from major suppliers like Howdens and Ikea (the latter requiring careful planning, as their units leave little room for services).

We also offer custom-built kitchens. Through our long-established relationship with a local joinery firm, we can supply specific component parts to the highest standard or build an entirely bespoke kitchen tailored to your space.

House Renovations

We carry out a wide variety of maintenance and renovation work. Older Bristol properties often require external maintenance at roof level—particularly parapet walls, which are susceptible to neglect and water incursions. We also address cracked or damaged rendering and can coordinate different trades if required.

Multi-skilled and experienced, we have a deep working knowledge of Bristol's housing stock and the issues that arise when modernising older buildings. Problem-solving is all part of a day's work. While there are always a few surprises, experience and a chat over a cuppa is a tried-and-tested method.

Please note: Structural engineers are required for all structural works and Building Control compliance.

Carpentry & Joinery

We undertake all aspects of carpentry and joinery, finished to the highest standard. Our close relationship with a local joinery firm allows us to supply bespoke items like sash windows, stairs, and skirting boards with matched mouldings.

  • Windows & Doors: Fitting or enlarging windows, fitting shutters and skylights, and hanging internal or external doors (off-the-shelf or made-to-measure).
  • Storage & Furniture: Building fitted or custom wardrobes, cupboards, bookshelves, desks, and commercial furniture.
  • Structural Woodwork: Building stairs, cut roofs, and fitted porches.
  • Flooring: Installation of timber, laminate, or tiled flooring to suit your budget and desired finish.

Period Renovations

This case study highlights a small part of the extensive internal and external renovation we carried out on a five-storey period property.

  • Sash Windows: Restored existing windows and built numerous new ones to match. We replaced rotten timbers, spliced in new sections, fitted draught-proof brushes, and adjusted the weights.
  • Exterior Works: Large-scale rendering with stainless steel mesh at the rear. The front was cleaned alongside a stonemason, using steam cleaners and spinners, followed by appropriate re-pointing.
  • Roofing & Interiors: Extensive lead work and parapet wall repairs, followed by a complete internal refurbishment. One highlight was restoring the first patent stove (though not to working order).

Outdoor Spaces

We build patios with step-free bi-fold doors, garden houses, summer houses, playhouses, and tree houses. Whether you want a functional outdoor area or a bespoke timber structure for the family, we design and build to suit your space.
