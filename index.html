Questions people ask us

What building and carpentry work do you do in Bristol?

Roofing including loft conversions and repairs, single storey extensions, kitchens, whole house renovations, carpentry and joinery, and outdoor spaces such as patios, summer houses and garden houses.

Do you work on Victorian houses?

Yes. We have extensive experience of Victorian housing, including knock-throughs as part of modernisation, new openings for patio doors, and dealing with the issues that older buildings can reveal during works.

Do you do commercial work as well as domestic?

Yes. We do commercial works for small and medium businesses, and building and carpentry works of all sizes for domestic customers.

Can you manage the whole project?

Yes, project management and scheduling can be included if you want it. We also work with local joiners, plasterers, electricians and plumbers to deliver a complete service without the overheads of a large company.
